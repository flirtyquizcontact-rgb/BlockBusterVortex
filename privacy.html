Privacy Policy

App name: Block Buster Vortex

Last updated: June 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Block Buster Vortex collects, uses, and protects information when you use the mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

Block Buster Vortex does not require users to create an account inside the app. However, the app may collect or process limited information through third-party services used for ads, analytics, crash reporting, cloud services, and gameplay features.

The app may process the following types of information:

  • Device information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, and language settings.
  • Advertising identifiers, when allowed by the user and applicable law.
  • Gameplay data, such as score, achievements, rewards, missions, progress, selected settings, selected music, selected background, and unlocked items.
  • Crash and diagnostic data used to improve app stability.
  • Approximate location may be inferred by advertising or analytics providers for legal, regional, or ad-delivery purposes.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and improve gameplay features.
  • Save game progress, settings, rewards, and achievements.
  • Display ads, including rewarded ads and limited interstitial ads.
  • Measure app performance and understand general usage trends.
  • Detect crashes and technical issues.
  • Support Google Play Games leaderboards and achievements, if the user chooses to use those features.
  • Send optional notifications, such as daily reward reminders, when notifications are enabled by the user.

3. Advertising and Consent

Block Buster Vortex uses Google AdMob to display ads. In regions where consent is required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage user consent choices.

Users may be shown rewarded ads to continue a game, open rewards, or receive optional bonuses. Ads are not required to play the basic game.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase Remote Config
  • Google Play Games Services

These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Local Data

The app may store game progress, high score, settings, selected music, selected background, rewards, missions, achievements, and other gameplay data locally on the user's device.

6. Notifications

The app may send optional notifications, such as daily reward reminders. On supported Android versions, users must grant notification permission before receiving notifications. Users can disable notifications at any time from the app settings or device settings.

7. Children

Block Buster Vortex is not intentionally directed to children under the age required by applicable law. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by third-party service providers listed above for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, cloud messaging, and gameplay services.

9. Data Retention

Local gameplay data remains on the device unless the user clears app data or uninstalls the app. Data processed by third-party services is retained according to those providers' policies.

10. User Choices

Users can:

  • Manage ad consent choices where applicable.
  • Disable notifications.
  • Clear local app data from device settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove locally stored data.
